Your Q3 payout

Cap: No one can earn more than 2× their quarterly CTC in a quarter (FY26-27 policy, clause 2). Quarterly CTC is annual CTC ÷ 4. This calculator assumes an annual CTC of ₹25 L for SMs and ₹35 L for TLs.

Streak bonus: Paid on top of the cap (clause 3). Hit 100% of target in all three months of Q3 to earn +25%.

Eligibility: Incentive is applicable only on bookings where the welcome call is done.

Incentive per month = total CV × payout % for your role × that month's multiplier. Spot incentives are not included.
